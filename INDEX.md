## Compatibility and provenance

Release archives come from the authors' CurseForge hosts or official GitHub releases. Updated archive manifests include Interface 16001. See [manifest.csv](manifest.csv) for sources and SHA-256 hashes and [verification.json](verification.json) for compatibility evidence. The original Places and Bosses archive record is retained as provenance for the unchanged SpokenBossesAudio 1.0.0; SpokenPlacesAudio now has its own 2.1.1 record.

Update verification (November 6): 4,164 installed files from eight updated packages and 1,771 files from EllesmereUI 9.7.8 were compared with the extracted releases. Saved settings were unchanged. Updated archives were checked for Forever manifests, executable entries, and unsafe paths; archive sizes and SHA-256 hashes are in the manifests. Unchanged archive records retain their original check dates. No in-game acceptance test was performed.
